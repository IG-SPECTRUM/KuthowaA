Why Satin?

Our premium high-density satin protects your skin, locks in your curls, and keeps you cool all night long.

Hair Health
Rough fibers cause friction, breakage, split ends, and bedhead frizz.
Friction-free slip that lets hair slide smoothly, preventing breakage.

Hydrophobic structure that keeps hydration and expensive skincare on your skin.
